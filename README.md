# When Forecasts Fail

## Investigating the Meteorological Drivers of Short-Term Forecast Uncertainty in the Carolinas

An independent undergraduate research project investigating the meteorological conditions associated with short-term weather forecast uncertainty across North Carolina and South Carolina.

### Status

Research design and data methodology currently under development.

### Research Question

What meteorological conditions are associated with the largest short-term temperature forecast errors across the Carolinas?

### Project Goals

- Evaluate historical forecast performance against observations
- Quantify forecast error and uncertainty
- Identify meteorological environments associated with larger errors
- Investigate significant forecast-error case studies
- Develop a reproducible Python-based analysis workflow

### Tools

- Pandas
